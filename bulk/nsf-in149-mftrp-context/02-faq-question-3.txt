What happens to proposals submitted between Oct. 10, 2025, and the delayed effective date set forth below? Proposals submitted during the appropriations lapse are not subject to the new requirements that were set to take effect on Oct. 10, 2025. Compliance is required for those policies in effect prior to the shutdown, namely the MFTRP certifications and FFDR submissions.
