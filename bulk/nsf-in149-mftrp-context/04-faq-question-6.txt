How are certifications documented in NSF proposal submissions? NSF IN-149 certifications occur within the proposal Cover Sheet, the Biographical Sketch, the Current and Pending (Other) Support form and Research.gov. Cover Sheet: Organizational certifications regarding research security training and the prohibitions on MFTRPs and Confucius Institutes. Biographical Sketch : Senior/key personnel certifications regarding research security training and the prohibition on participation in MFTRPs. Current and Pending (Other) Support : Senior/key personnel certifications regarding research security training and the prohibition on participation in MFTRPs. Research .gov: PI and co-PI annual certification regarding the prohibition on participation in MFTRPs and organizational certification regarding annual FFDR submission.
