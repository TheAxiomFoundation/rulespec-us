# Atomic scope for 45 CFR 604.100

Encode only paragraphs (a)-(c) for Federal contracts, grants, loans, and
cooperative agreements. Paragraphs (d)-(e), concerning loan insurance and loan
guarantees, are outside this atomic slice.

1. Paragraph (a) prohibits a recipient from expending appropriated funds to pay
   any person for influencing or attempting to influence a listed agency or
   Congressional actor in connection with the linked award, making, entry,
   extension, continuation, renewal, amendment, or modification of one of the
   four covered instruments. This prohibition has no dollar threshold.
2. Paragraph (b) supplies the separate Appendix A certification component. Do
   not emit a final thresholdless filing schedule from paragraph (b); section
   604.110 supplies the transaction thresholds and filing occasions.
3. Paragraph (c) supplies the separate conditional disclosure trigger: the
   requester or recipient made or agreed to make a nonappropriated-fund payment
   (including profits from a covered Federal action) that would be prohibited
   by paragraph (a) if appropriated funds paid it. Such nonappropriated-fund
   lobbying is not itself prohibited by paragraph (a). It conditionally
   requires the Appendix B disclosure form/SF-LLL, subject to section 604.110's
   filing occasions and thresholds.

Do not defer paragraph (a). Do not amount-gate it. Do not omit Federal loans.
Do not merge certification and conditional disclosure. The payment and the
requester's or recipient's covered transaction must be explicitly linked.
