# Atomic scope for Appendix A to 45 CFR Part 604

Encode only the certification for Federal contracts, grants, loans, and
cooperative agreements, not the distinct statement for loan guarantees or loan
insurance.

- Keep the certification's no-past-or-future Federal-appropriated-funds
  lobbying attestation separate from its conditional SF-LLL disclosure clause.
- The SF-LLL clause applies only when other-than-Federal-appropriated funds have
  been or will be paid for influencing a listed official in connection with the
  same covered transaction. Do not characterize that payment as prohibited.
- Separately encode the undersigned's duty to include the certification
  language in award documents for all subawards at all tiers, and the stated
  duty that subrecipients certify and disclose accordingly. Preserve the
  undersigned/subrecipient roles and linked award documents/subawards.
- Do not turn evidence of completed certification language, completed
  disclosure, or completed flow-down into a tautological compliance output.
  Encode obligations or triggers whose truth is determined by source-stated
  facts.
- Do not encode the civil-penalty text or any discretionary enforcement.
- Appendix A does not replace section 604.110's instrument-specific thresholds,
  lower-tier filing rule, or disclosure-only forwarding path.
