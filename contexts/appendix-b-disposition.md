archived official images: the SF-LLL form, its instructions, and continuation
sheet. It proves the identity of Appendix B as the prescribed "Disclosure Form
To Report Lobbying." It does not provide machine-readable text supporting a new
trigger, amount threshold, actor inference, transaction-linkage inference, or
form-completeness rule. Those duties come from sections 604.100 and 604.110 and
Appendix A. Reject a standalone executable Appendix B candidate rather than
